Privacy policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

This policy explains how the Glade Samsung TV player handles information. Glade is free for this release and supplies no channels, media, provider account or subscription.

Who is responsible

Glade is operated by Martin Hafizi. For privacy questions or requests, contact hafizi.martin@gmail.com. This policy covers the current Samsung TV player and this privacy website. A separately hosted browser player may involve additional server processing and needs its own deployment disclosures.

Information used on your TV

You enter a provider address, username and password. You may also enter a playlist label and optional endpoint URL. Glade saves provider profiles and the selected profile in the app’s local storage so you can reconnect. Local profile identifiers and save times help manage those profiles.

The app also stores favorites, recently played titles, movie and episode playback progress, interface language and view preferences on the device. Media identifiers, titles and artwork addresses can be included in these records. These features personalize your library and enable resume playback. The reviewed TV build has no developer-operated account database or cloud synchronization for these records.

Network connections and recipients

Glade sends the credentials and catalogue or playback requests needed to the provider you choose. The provider, its streaming servers and artwork hosts can receive your IP address, ordinary connection information, requested media identifiers and authentication data where the request requires them. They may process data in other countries. Their operators, locations and retention practices depend on the provider and URLs you configure; consult that provider’s privacy information before connecting.

When diagnosing a connection failure, the current build may send an HTTPS connectivity request to Google’s DNS service at dns.google for the fixed name example.com. This is not a request for the name of your provider. Google can receive normal network information such as your IP address. Some artwork fallback paths can request an image from dummyimage.com with a title or fallback label in the URL; other paths render missing artwork locally.

Glade does not include an advertising or developer analytics service in the reviewed TV build and does not sell personal information. Samsung’s TV, store and operating-system services are governed by Samsung’s own notices. Optional local developer diagnostic builds are separate from the standard release and must not be distributed as that release.

Storage and connection security

Saved provider credentials are stored in app-local storage; Glade does not add application-level encryption to that storage. Access is subject to the device’s protections. Use only a provider you trust and an HTTPS provider endpoint where supported. The app also accepts HTTP endpoints; HTTP does not encrypt credentials or stream requests in transit. Provider credentials can appear in the URLs required by the provider’s API and stream format. Never send those URLs in public reports or screenshots.

Retention and deletion

Saved profiles remain until removed or local app data is cleared. In Settings, remove a saved playlist or choose Log out all to remove the saved provider credentials. Log out all also clears recently played history, but it does not currently clear favorites, resume progress, language or view preferences. Favorites can be removed individually. Playback progress is capped at 200 entries and favorites at 100; these limits are not time-based deletion schedules.

To remove all remaining local records, use the platform’s app-data reset or removal options where available; the procedure and behavior depend on the TV. Contact us for guidance. We cannot remotely access or erase your TV’s local storage, and deleting local records does not delete a provider’s server records. Ask the provider about its retention and deletion procedures.

If you email us, your email service and ours process the correspondence. We use the message, your address and information you choose to provide to answer your request. Do not send provider passwords. We retain correspondence only as needed to handle the request and any applicable legal obligations; ask the contact above about access or deletion.

Your choices and rights

Providing provider credentials is necessary to connect that provider; without them, its catalogue and streams cannot be used. Favorites and interface preferences support the features you choose to use. You may stop using Glade, remove profiles and manage local records as described above.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, erase or receive your personal data, restrict or object to processing, or withdraw consent where consent applies. Contact Martin Hafizi at the email above. We may need proportionate information to verify a request. Rights relating to data held independently by a provider must also be directed to that provider. You may complain to your competent data-protection authority.

Children

Glade has no child-account or parental-consent workflow and does not knowingly seek personal information from children. A parent or guardian should supervise a child’s use of provider accounts and media. Contact us if you believe a child has supplied personal information to us.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages and serves the English and Korean policies without a Glade login, advertising scripts, analytics scripts or forms. GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security purposes; its hosting services also process connection information needed to deliver pages. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for its practices. Website hosting records are distinct from the TV’s locally saved playlists and viewing records. Visiting this website does not upload those TV records.

Changes

We will update the date and this policy when the described practices change. Material changes requiring additional notice or consent will be addressed before the relevant processing begins. English and Korean versions describe the same current practices; report any translation discrepancy to the contact above.
